Recommendations

What would help

311 already responds fairly once a problem is reported. These twelve steps aim at the gap before a request exists: making sure every community is heard. Most can be piloted within existing budgets.

Group A

Measure who 311 hears

Today 311 reports how fast it responds. It doesn't report who it's hearing from, so the gap in Finding 1 stays invisible.

Publish reports per 1,000 residents as an equity measure

Add reports per 1,000 residents by community and ward to 311's quarterly performance reporting, next to response time.

Group B

Make reporting easier

The biggest gaps are where language and familiarity with the system are the biggest barriers. Reporting should not require English or typing.

3

Launch the multilingual app where it matters most

Roll out the 311 app's new languages first in high-gap communities, with in-language promotion through local media, settlement agencies and faith centres.

Lead
311 & Insights; Marketing & Communications
Effort
Medium
How we'd know it works
App use in high-gap communities rises toward the city average (Finding 3).
4

Add photo-first reporting

Snap a photo, drop a pin and pick an icon. No typing and no English needed, with the request routed by the photo's location and the chosen icon.

Lead
311 & Insights
Effort
Medium
How we'd know it works
Share of requests made photo-first; new reporters in high-gap communities.
5

Accept reports by text or WhatsApp

A number residents can message in the most-spoken languages of high-gap communities, routed into the same 311 queue.

Lead
311 & Insights
Effort
Medium
How we'd know it works
Messages received and converted to valid requests.
6

Close the loop

Tell every reporter when the problem is fixed, with a photo where possible. People report again when they see results.

Lead
Service departments
Effort
Low
How we'd know it works
Repeat reporting rate; resident satisfaction with 311.
Group C

Reward and encourage reporting

Small, well-designed incentives and trusted local messengers can turn reporting into a habit in communities that report least.

7

Fix-It Points

Verified, non-duplicate infrastructure reports earn points redeemable for City-owned rewards such as leisure centre drop-in passes or transit day passes.

Lead
311 & Insights; Recreation
Effort
Medium
How we'd know it works

Group D

Don't rely on reports alone

Even with better reporting, some problems will go unreported. The City can find them directly.

11

How Fix-It Points would work

Earn
Points for each infrastructure report the City verifies as real and not a duplicate. Bonus points for a first report from a new household in a high-gap community.
Redeem
City-owned rewards only: leisure centre drop-in passes, transit day passes, or a donation of points to the community association.
Safeguards
Points awarded only after verification; a monthly cap per person; no points for duplicates; a fixed annual reward budget.
Where first
High-gap communities only during the pilot, so rewards reach the people 311 hears from least.

Incentives carry risks: duplicate or trivial reports, and rewards flowing to residents who already report. Verification before points are awarded, monthly caps and a pilot limited to high-gap communities address most of these. The City's own data would show quickly whether verified reports per resident rise.

A 12-month pilot the City could run

Where
Two or three wards with large reporting gaps (for example Wards 3, 5 and 9), with similar communities elsewhere as a comparison group.
What
In-language and photo-first reporting promoted locally, Fix-It Points, a Community Care Challenge and trained ambassadors.
Measures
Verified infrastructure reports per 1,000 residents; share of new reporters; duplicate rate; defects found by proactive inspection; awareness of 311.
Success test
Reporting in pilot communities closes at least half the gap with similar communities, with no rise in the duplicate rate.
Cost control
Rewards limited to City-owned passes within a fixed budget; ambassadors through existing settlement-agency partnerships.
