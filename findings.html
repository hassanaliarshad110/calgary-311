Findings

What the 311 data shows

Finding 1

Communities where more residents don't speak English report far fewer problems

We grouped communities into four equal groups by the share of residents with no knowledge of English (2021 Census), then counted reports about roads, sidewalks, streetlights, water, sewers, parks and trees per 1,000 residents. These include snow and ice, debris and park garbage requests. Counting only defects gives the same result.

Infrastructure reports per 1,000 residents per year

Part of that raw gap comes from other differences: older neighbourhoods have older pipes and sidewalks, and newer suburbs have fewer problems. Comparing communities first settled in the same era, the gap remains. Those groups still differ in income and location, which the estimate below accounts for.

Like for like: 63 communities first settled 1975–2000
Every way we measured it shows fewer reports

Best estimate: about 23% fewer reports (95% range 9% to 33%) after accounting for income, renters, community age and distance from downtown. The pattern holds outside northeast Calgary, for roads, water and parks separately, in each year studied, and when communities are linked to areas using the City's own community lists (about 20% fewer).

One caution: communities with more non-English speakers also tend to have more visible-minority residents and recent newcomers. The data can't separate these, so this shows who 311 hears less from, not exactly why.

Finding 2

Once a problem is reported, the City responds equally fast everywhere

We compared each community's requests with the citywide speed for the same type of request in the same year. Across income groups and language groups, the share closed within 7 days is within 1.5% of expected, and within 0.6% at 30 days. Long waits of more than 90 days are rare (about 2% of requests) and vary more, from 93% to 107% of expected across income groups, with no consistent direction.

Requests closed within 7 days, compared with the citywide expectation (100%)

One caution: "closed" in 311 records means the City closed the request, which doesn't always mean the problem was fixed.
