Who Calgary's 311 hears

Calgary's 311 system responds just as quickly in poorer neighbourhoods as in richer ones. But it hears far less from neighbourhoods where many residents don't speak English. In a system that fixes what people report, problems nobody reports can go unfixed.

What we found

What would help

  1. Track reports per resident as a fairness measure. 311 already tracks response time. Tracking how often each community reports would show where the system isn't hearing from people.
  2. Launch the multilingual 311 app where it's needed most. Promote it first in communities with the largest reporting gaps, through settlement agencies, faith centres, schools and community associations.
  3. Check, don't wait. In under-reporting communities, add proactive inspection of roads, sidewalks, streetlights and parks. The City has already tested AI that spots road defects from vehicle cameras.
  4. Measure "fixed", not just "closed". Report how often a closed request is followed by a new one for the same issue.
